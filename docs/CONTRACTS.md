# Onchain components

Both contracts are participation infrastructure. The intelligence layer reads Arc; it does not need
these contracts to work.

## ArcwayPassport

`contracts/src/ArcwayPassport.sol`

- Records verified quest completions (`completeQuest(user, questId, xp)`) and badges (`awardBadge`).
- Each quest counts once per wallet. XP values live in `lib/quests.ts`.
- Events (`QuestCompleted`) feed the leaderboard, the proof panel and the optional Envio indexer.

Trust model:

- Only allow-listed verifiers can record. The verifier checks activity on Arc first (`lib/server/verify.ts`).
- A verifier can record qualifying activity. It cannot move user funds.
- Ownership transfer is two-step (`transferOwnership`, `acceptOwnership`).

## ArcwayCampaigns

`contracts/src/ArcwayCampaigns.sol`

A project funds a USDC prize pool; people who complete the tasks join for free and share it.

| Mode | Payout |
|---|---|
| First come | Fixed reward per eligible entrant, paid on entry, until the pool runs out |
| Prize draw | Winners drawn after the end time; everyone has the same chance |
| Shared pool | Pool split equally between everyone who joined |

Rules enforced by the contract:

- Duration 1 hour to 90 days; start at most 90 days ahead; claim window 7 to 90 days.
- Entry requires an EIP-712 signature from the verifier over `(campaignId, user, deadline)`, issued
  only after the tasks are checked on Arc.
- Draw and split results are published as a Merkle root. Leaf:
  `keccak256(bytes.concat(keccak256(abi.encode(id, user, amount))))`, sorted-pair hashing.
- Draw seed is the hash of the first block at or after the end time; winners are the lowest
  `keccak(seed, address)`, so anyone can recompute them. Results are built only when the entrant
  list matches the onchain entry count.
- Refunds to the project: cancelled before start, ended with no entries, results more than 3 days
  late, or unclaimed after the claim window.

Trust model:

- The project funds the pool; funds stay in the contract, separate from Arcway's operating funds.
- The verifier can admit entrants and publish a root that anyone can check. It cannot withdraw pools.

## Verifier key

One server-only key (`VERIFIER_PRIVATE_KEY`) signs passport records, campaign entries and results.
It never gets a `NEXT_PUBLIC_` name and needs only a little USDC for fees.
