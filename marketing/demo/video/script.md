# Arcway demo voiceover

Voice: en-NG-AbeoNeural, rate -5%. One audio file per section. Timestamps are where each section starts.

## 1. Hook [0:00]
You just landed on Arc with some USDC, and nothing tells you what to try first.

## 2. Who it's for [0:06]
Arcway is for people new to Arc, like a student in Lagos who just bridged ten dollars, and for the app teams who want those people to actually use their product.

## 3. Feature one, find apps and take first steps [0:17]
Start on Explore. Every Arc app is listed by category, with the contracts its team publishes and how active they are on chain. Then open Quests. Seventeen short quests in four tiers walk you from holding USDC to using apps regularly. Arcway checks each step against your real activity before it counts.

## 4. Feature two, sponsored quests [0:40]
Here is the new part. Any app or community can put USDC behind a quest. This card says it pays ten cents. On the sponsor page, you pick the quest, the reward per person, how many people, and how long it runs. The contract holds the money, not us.

## 5. Feature three, a real payout on mainnet [1:01]
This is Arc mainnet with real USDC. A sponsor escrowed half a dollar for five people behind the first quest. A wallet that completed it tapped claim. The contract checked its record and paid it directly. Both transactions are on the Arc explorer, and the sponsor page counts every payout. When a bounty ends, whatever nobody claimed goes back to the sponsor.

## 6. The tech [1:28]
Under the hood, the ArcwayBounties contract reads one thing from the Arcway Passport contract. Has this wallet completed this quest? There is no admin key, no fee and no server in the payout path. We built it with Arc Studio. Studio read our repo, wrote the contract and its deploy script, ran the tests, audited our contracts and deployed everything to testnet. Later it built the sponsor tools, top up and close and refund. We reviewed every change, grew the tests to forty six, and shipped the same code to mainnet.

## 7. Impact and call to action [2:05]
For apps, it is an onboarding budget that only pays for people who really used the product. For new users, it is a reason to take a first step on Arc and get paid for it. Next, we are bringing Arc app teams on to sponsor first steps in their own apps. Try it at arcway dot world, and if you build on Arc, sponsor a quest.

End [2:30]
