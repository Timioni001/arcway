# App logos

Logos belong to their respective projects and are shown only to identify each
app in the directory. They are unmodified copies from the open
[DefiLlama icons](https://github.com/DefiLlama/icons) collection. If a project
publishes an official brand kit, prefer its SVG and replace the file here.

Apps without a confirmed logo (Tower Exchange, Rain, Arc Portal) show their
category icon instead of a placeholder.
